WHAT IS PANTHERS FUND

Panthers Fund is an autonomous AI trading fund operating on the Base blockchain. The agent manages a diversified crypto portfolio across three allocation buckets, making trading decisions through LLM-driven analysis of market conditions, technical indicators, and sentiment data.

Each NFT share represents proportional ownership of the fund's total value. Net Asset Value (NAV) is recalculated continuously based on the fund's trading pool performance. The agent operates 24/7 without human intervention.

HOW TO BUY

1
Connect your MetaMask wallet using the button in the top right corner. Make sure you are on Base Sepolia network.
2
Navigate to the My NFTs tab. Click "Buy an NFT Share" at the fixed price, or make a custom offer that the agent will evaluate.
3
Approve the USDC transfer from your connected wallet within the 30-minute window. The agent detects payment automatically on Base and creates your NFT share.

HOW OWNERSHIP WORKS

Panthers Fund NFTs are standard Solana NFTs. Ownership is wallet-native — whoever holds the NFT in their wallet owns that share of the fund. NFTs are freely tradeable on any Solana NFT marketplace.

NAV per share is calculated as total fund TVL divided by the number of outstanding NFTs. When the fund's trading pool grows, each share's NAV increases proportionally.

VERIFYING THE AGENT

The agent's identity is registered on-chain via the ERC-8004 registry on Base Sepolia:

0x8004A818BFB912233c491871b3d84c89A494BD9e

Mainnet registration will follow. The registry provides cryptographic proof that the agent controlling the fund wallet is the same entity registered on-chain.

panthers.fund is a convenience frontend. The canonical data lives on-chain — the agent's Solana wallet, NFT mint addresses, and USDC balances are all publicly verifiable through any Solana explorer. This dashboard reads from the agent's API, which reads from the chain.
